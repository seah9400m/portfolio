I am Seada Hassen, a lecturer in the Department of Environmental Health at Wollo University. My work bridges public health, environmental science, and food safety — training students to identify, prevent, and manage the environmental factors that affect human health.

My teaching philosophy is grounded in practical, evidence-based learning. I believe students learn best when theory is connected to real-world scenarios — from water quality testing and sanitation inspections to food safety audits and community health education.

Beyond the classroom, I am engaged in research on environmental health, food safety systems, and community hygiene practices. I also supervise student research projects and participate in outreach programs that translate science into community action.
